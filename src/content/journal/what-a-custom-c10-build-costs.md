People ask for “a C10 build” like it is one product. It is not. The number depends on the truck you start with and how far you want to go.

## Honest ranges

These are shop-world numbers, not Instagram comments.

- **Driver refresh:** brakes, steering, cooling, suspension, and the ugly mechanicals. Keep the paint. Think tens of thousands, not a weekend.
- **Restomod driver:** LS or equivalent, better chassis or a quality aftermarket setup, decent interior, paint that can live outside. This is where most real trucks land.
- **Show-and-go:** one-off metal, custom paint, full interior, sorted air or coils, and no visible shortcuts. This is the number that surprises people.

A cheap shell is not a cheap build. Rust repair, missing trim, and a twisted frame will eat the “deal” before the first LS crate shows up.

## What moves the number

1. **The body.** Rust, prior accidents, and how original the panels are.
2. **The chassis.** Stock with upgrades vs a full aftermarket frame.
3. **The powertrain.** A clean LS swap is not the same job as a boosted setup with a custom tunnel.
4. **Paint.** Bodywork is the silent budget killer.
5. **Interior and wiring.** The last 20% takes the last 40% of the money.

## How to not waste it

Bring a truck with a straight cab. Decide stance and power before anyone cuts. Budget a contingency, because every C10 hides something.
